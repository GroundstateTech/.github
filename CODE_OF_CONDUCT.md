# Groundstate Community Conduct

Groundstate welcomes technical disagreement, experimentation, criticism, and competing approaches.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people; avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and deliberate exposure of sensitive information; respect project scope; disclose materially relevant conflicts of interest; and avoid knowingly false claims about security, safety, authorship, research results, or project affiliation.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct materially disrupts safe, lawful, or productive collaboration.
