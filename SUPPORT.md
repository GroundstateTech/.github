# Supporting Groundstate Technology LLC

Groundstate develops open-source software, experimental prototypes, research tools, documentation, and selected private R&D.

Financial support may help cover development time, testing hardware, equipment, hosting, domains, research materials, accessibility testing, documentation, and field/community work.

## What support does not provide

Unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise, financial support does not purchase:

- equity or membership interests in Groundstate Technology LLC;
- ownership of Groundstate intellectual property;
- control over project governance or roadmap decisions;
- exclusive licensing rights;
- certification, endorsement, or validation of a product or research result;
- priority access to private data, security information, or confidential R&D.

Thank you for helping us keep useful work moving.
